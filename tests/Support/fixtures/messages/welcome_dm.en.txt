✨ Welcome to the Test Crew crew! ✨

Our nights happen because people like you lend a hand.

🌟 Every task you take keeps the door open, the room ready and the music going.

🎟️ And every task earns you free entry.

This message explains how it all works. To see your numbers, tickets or credits, just ask the bot in a private chat.

🧩 The tasks

🎈 Decorate: Set up before the dance: close the curtains, check the bathrooms, place the LED candles, decorate the DJ table, prepare the water and fruit, and open the movable walls.

🙋 Welcome: Welcome people at the door, check and sell tickets, and count how many people come in.

🧹 Clean: Pack up after the dance: collect the candles and decorations, clear away the water and snacks, empty full bins, close the movable walls, open the curtains, and do the final check.

🎟️ Tickets

When you sign up for a task, you get a free ticket for that night. Show it at the door.

🎁 Credits

You earn 1 free-entry credit for every 2 tasks you complete. You then get a ticket for the night you choose. Some nights (sold out, or special events) do not accept credits.

📈 Standing

Your standing comes from how your past tasks went:

🔹 Completed the task: 100%

🔹 Arrived late, did the task: 90%

🔹 Found a replacement: 80%

🔹 Cancelled less than 48 hours before: 40%

🔹 No-show: 0%

Cancelling 48 hours or more before has no effect. Recent tasks count more than old ones. You get a rating after 3 finished tasks. 60% or higher is good standing. Below 60%, you cannot sign up for new tasks until you talk to an organizer.

💬 Talking to the bot

In a private chat with the bot, it is usually enough to write what you want in your own words. There are no commands or keywords to remember.

Any difficulty, feedback or a bug? Just tell the bot: it passes it on to the organizers.

If you cannot reach the bot, contact the maintainer, maintainer@example.org or @maintainer, or just try again a bit later.
