EMAIL Organizer guide: how EventCrew works

Hi Person,

You are an organizer of Test Crew. This guide explains how EventCrew works, first in short and then in detail.

💡 You don’t have to learn the commands: they are all under the bot’s menu button. You can also just write to the bot what you need, in your own words. It usually understands, in most languages.

⚡ In short

- 🗓️ Tasks: you create them in wp-admin, EventCrew > Tasks. One click makes all the tasks of a night.
- ✋ Sign-up: members tap a task on the board in the Telegram group, or use the web sign-up page. /board posts a new board in the group.
- 🌙 On the night: /roster marks who arrived, who completed their task and who did not come. The sales desk link sells tickets and checks people in at the door; /door, then 💳 Sales desk, sends you the link.
- 🎁 Credits: members earn 1 free-entry credit per 2 completed tasks. /people, then 🎁 Give credits, gives credits by hand.
- 📈 Standing: 60% or higher is good standing. It comes from how each member's past tasks went.
- 🔑 Roles: /people, then 🏷 Roles, organizers and passes, makes someone an organizer, gives a one-time pass past the sign-up gate, and gives or takes away roles such as DJ.
- 🎧 Role tasks (DJ, VJ, Organize, ...) are not on the group board. People with the role see them with /board in a private chat and in the Crew app. You can add someone there, even a person who is not a crew member.
- 🔔 Messages: reminders and calls go out by themselves, only between 09:00 and 21:00.
- ⚙️ wp-admin, EventCrew > Settings changes how things work. EventCrew > Diagnostics shows whether everything is running.

🗓️ Tasks and events

A role is a kind of job, for example Welcome or Clean. You set the roles in Settings: name, emoji, a short description, how many people, and when it starts and ends compared to the event.

A task is one role on one date. In EventCrew > Tasks you create tasks one by one, or all the tasks of a night at once from the roles. A task can be linked to an event, so it shows under the event's name. A task set to 0 people stays saved but leaves the board.

In a private chat with the bot you can do the same: /addtask adds a task, /edittask changes its time, places, notes or plus-ones, /deletetask deletes it, /maketasks creates a night's tasks from the roles, and /mailtasks sends the open-task email now. The bot asks you to confirm first.

📣 The board and sign-up

The board in the Telegram group lists the open tasks under each event and date. Each task is a button that shows its role, time and places taken; a member taps it to sign up. It updates itself whenever something changes. /board in the group posts a new copy at the bottom. Settings > Telegram bot > Refresh the board redraws it in place.

A new member sends /start to the bot in a private chat and confirms their email address. On the web sign-up page they sign in with a link sent to their email. Both follow the same rules.

If a language model is set up in Settings > Telegram bot, the bot also answers plain messages. In a private chat it answers questions about tasks, events, the site's pages and the official email address (the contact in Settings > Privacy), and can sign a member up for a task or take them off one; it asks them to confirm with a button first. It remembers a private chat for 24 hours, and in the group what each person wrote to it, separately, also for 24 hours. It knows each person's roles and leaves out what is meant only for others. When an organizer writes to it privately, it also knows this guide and the infrastructure note. In the group it answers only when the message is meant for the bot and it knows the answer. When someone in a private chat gives feedback, is unhappy or very happy, or asks for a human, every organizer gets that chat by email and a short Telegram message, at most once per person per hour (switch it off in Settings > Telegram bot).

Cancelling less than 48 hours before the task counts as a late cancellation. A member who cannot come can find a replacement: the replacement sends /tasks to the bot, taps 🔁 Take over a task, and the member accepts.

🌙 On the night: roster and sales desk

/roster in a private chat with the bot shows the night's crew. Mark each person arrived, arrived late, completed or no-show, one by one or a whole crew at once. EventCrew > Roster in wp-admin does the same for any date. The night's welcome crew can mark the crew too.

The sales desk is a web page for the door. It shows tonight's list: the crew, members with a free-entry ticket, guests and Holvi ticket buyers. You mark people in and out and say how many people they brought. Add sells a ticket at the door, adds a guest, or adds a member who spends a credit or takes one of tonight's tasks.

When you are signed up for the night's Organize task, you get the sales desk link 30 minutes before the night's first task, with a button to the Admin tab. The welcome crew (people on tasks of a role marked "Opens the sales desk") get it once they are marked arrived. /door, then 💳 Sales desk, sends a new link any time. /door has buttons for the door list (who has a ticket) and the guest list.

Holvi ticket buyers: in EventCrew > Roster, pick the date and paste the names, one per line, under "Holvi ticket buyers". A name already on the list is skipped.

After each import, every imported ticket with an email address is emailed to its buyer automatically: a thank-you message with the ticket's QR code, in every site language, or in one language when its Hybrid tick in Settings > Email is off. Roster and the Admin tab show how many were sent, failed or not sent yet. "Send a test to me" sends one to your own address; "Send the rest now" sends every ticket not sent yet, failed ones too.

Every send waits 10 minutes before anything goes out, whether an import or "Send the rest now" started it. Until then, Roster and the Admin tab show the time, how many get an email, and a "Cancel sending" button. Cancelling sends nothing; those tickets stay not sent. "Send a test to me" is not delayed.

The same ticket email goes to a guest you add with an email address (wp-admin Roster, the Admin tab, or /door add Name name@example.com) and to a Holvi buyer added at the sales desk with an email address, with the next batch. Crew get a separate ticket email when they take a task or spend a credit. When a guest is removed, or a task or credit is cancelled, after the ticket was emailed, the person gets a short "Your ticket is cancelled" email.

🎟️ Tickets, credits and standing

Signing up for a task gives the member a free ticket for that night. The ticket is a web page with a clock that ticks. A clock that does not move is a screenshot.

Members earn 1 free-entry credit per 2 completed tasks and spend it with /me, 🎟 Tickets, 🎟 Get a free-entry ticket (or /tickets), or on the web page. You give credits with /people, on the People page, or at the sales desk. Each gift is written in the credit log on the People page, and the member is told.

- 🎫 One working ticket per person per night, for all their tasks. Why: one entry per person, and the door sees all their tasks at once.
- 🔒 The working ticket's code never changes. Why: screenshots and saved QR codes keep working.
- 🎟️ Members can't cancel a free-entry ticket; organizers still can (/unticket). Why: one simple rule, and nobody can win a credit back by changing plans late.
- 💳 A bought ticket never becomes a credit, and taking a task on that night changes nothing. Why: it was paid for, and credits have no cash value.
- ➕ An extra task on the same night earns one more credit, pending until the after-event run adds it for a task marked completed or arrived late. Why: credit only for work actually done.

Free-entry credits and tickets have no cash value. They can't be refunded, sold or paid out in any form; they can only be used to get a ticket for an eligible event.

Standing is worked out again each time from how a member's past tasks went; recent tasks count more. A member gets a rating after 3 finished tasks. 60% or higher is good standing. Settings > Reputation holds the weights and limits.

🔑 Roles and permissions

A role can have conditions in EventCrew > Settings: how many tasks of which roles a member has completed. Meeting them does not give the role. You are told, by Telegram and by email, the first time someone meets a role's conditions and the first time someone is given a role. /people, then ✅ Who may hold each role, lists who meets the conditions of each role.

As an organizer you can add anyone to any task of a night, also one for a role they do not hold (they then get the role), and an organizer to Organize: in a private chat with /assign task ; name (/unassign takes them off) or ➕ Add someone under /board, in the Crew app Admin tab, or at the sales desk.

/people, in a private chat with the bot, also gives a one-time pass past the sign-up gate, makes someone an organizer (or stops them being one), and gives or takes away roles such as DJ or VJ. The People page in wp-admin and the Admin tab in the Crew app can do the same.

💬 Commands in a private chat

In a private chat with the bot you can do several crew page tasks by command. Write the name as you remember it: the bot finds the person, asks with buttons when several fit, and shows every change with ✅ and ✖️ first. A date first, like /mark 14.11. Milla ; done, picks another night.

For a person: /mark name ; arrived, late, noshow or done sets the roster mark. /standing name shows standing, credits and log. /newcomer shows a newcomer waiting for your OK. /ticket name spends a credit on free entry, /unticket name gives it back. /unban name lifts a block.

For a night: /freeentry close or /freeentry open closes or reopens free entry. /stop announcement stops the next announcement, /stop email the post-event email. /newboard posts a new board in the group. /sendtickets sends the ticket emails now, /sendtickets cancel stops a waiting send.

/tell task ; message sends your message to everyone on that task: by Telegram in their own language, under a one-line header, or by email when they have no Telegram. You see it first. You can send 5 an hour.

Every member can use /crew to see who works with them on their next nights, and /name or /email to change their own name or email. A new email changes only after they open the link sent to it.

🔔 Automatic messages

- 🔹 Sign-up and cancellation confirmations, at once.
- 🔹 A reminder 24 hours before each task.
- 🔹 A call to all members when tasks still need people.
- 🔹 Notices about standing, credits, tickets and permissions.
- 🔹 The sales desk link on the night.
- 🔹 A welcome message to each new member when they confirm their email, and this guide to each organizer.

The scheduled ones go out only between 09:00 and 21:00. Each goes by Telegram and by email. Members choose which ones they get with /settings, 🔔 Choose notifications; confirmations and reminders always go.

📰 The next events announcement, if an administrator has switched it on, emails the coming events to everyone who joined it. Before it goes out, each organizer gets a preview email with a button to stop it; the Crew app Admin tab has the same button. To add your own text to the next announcement, send /newsletter followed by the text in a private chat with the bot, or tell the bot there in your own words what to add (it shows the text with a Confirm button first), or use the Admin tab. Write it in any language: it is translated into every site language.

💌 The post-event email, if an administrator has switched it on, goes the day after each event at 12:00 to everyone at the event who gave an email address, the crew on its tasks too: a thank-you to those who came (checked in at the door, or marked Arrived or Completed on the roster), and a kind "sorry we missed you" to those who did not. Both show the next three events. At 12:00 the day before the event, each organizer gets a preview of both with a button to stop it; the Roster page and the Crew app Admin tab have the same button and a "Send a test to me" button. The Roster page shows how many were sent and how many people opened a link.

✏️ The thank-you text and the sorry text of the post-event email are set in wp-admin under EventCrew > Messages. To use another text for the next post-event email only, send /followup came or /followup missed followed by the text in a private chat with the bot, or tell the bot there in your own words (it shows the text with a Confirm button first), or use the Crew app Admin tab. Write it in any language: it is translated into every site language. After that email is sent, or when that night is stopped, the text on the Messages page is used again. Send /followup alone to see both texts.

📸 /events, in a private chat with the bot, shows you the next 5 coming events as cards: the picture, the date, the price, a short description, and buttons to buy a ticket and to listen to the DJ. Tap Confirm and the bot posts the same cards in the group; tap Cancel and nothing is sent. You can also ask the bot in your own words which events come next.

🤖 AI in EventCrew

EventCrew uses an AI language model only when its API URL and model are set in Settings > Telegram bot, under "Plain messages (language model)". Only the texts below go to that AI service, and without the URL or the model none of this happens. The ticket file import by email does not use AI.

No AI service is set up at the moment, so nothing is sent to one.

- 💬 Plain messages to the bot: the message, the last 24 hours of that chat, and the open tasks, events and chosen site pages, to answer and to see what the person wants. Every change (a sign-up, a cancellation, the guest list, your own email text) is shown first with Confirm and Cancel. Off: untick "Answer plain messages with the language model".
- 📰 Next events announcement and post-event email: the description of each event and of its DJs, to write short event texts and the opening text, and to pick out the venue, address and door times. All of it is in the preview email first: stop it there, or add your own text with /newsletter, /followup or the Admin tab. Off: untick "Texts by the language model" in Settings > Email.
- ✍️ AI texts for each event: the event and your prompt, for each message with "Write a new text with the AI for each event" ticked on the Messages page, and for your own placeholders. They are written the day before the event; read or change them before they go out in EventCrew > Messages > Coming events, or under AI texts in the Crew app Admin tab. Untick to stop.
- 🎧 The group board: the DJs and description of the next event, for two sentences under its picture. This text has no preview and no switch of its own.
- 🌍 Translation: the texts you write (message texts, your own email texts, the Translations page) are translated into every site language when saved. You can change any language by hand, and a text changed by hand is not overwritten. Off: untick "Translate texts automatically with the language model" in EventCrew > Translations.
- 📝 What the AI is asked to do is set in EventCrew > Messages, AI tab, where you can change it.

⚙️ The wp-admin pages

- 🔹 Tasks: create, change and remove tasks.
- 🔹 People: members, their history and credits, the credit log, organizers, and the Role conditions tab.
- 🔹 Roster: attendance and the door list for any date, and the Holvi ticket buyers.
- 🔹 Settings: roles, reputation, notifications and their times, the Telegram bot, email, privacy, and the welcome message.
- 🔹 Messages: the text of every email and Telegram message, the AI prompts, the texts for the coming events, and how every email looks.
- 🔹 Bot log: which bot commands and buttons each person used on a day. Message text is not stored.
- 🔹 Diagnostics: a check of the database, bot, scheduled sends, sign-up page and mail.
