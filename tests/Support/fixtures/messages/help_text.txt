How the Test Crew crew works. Your own numbers are in the Me tab, and the bot shows them when you ask in a private chat.

## 🧩 The tasks

- 🎈 Decorate: Set up before the dance: close the curtains, check the bathrooms, place the LED candles, decorate the DJ table, prepare the water and fruit, and open the movable walls.
- 🙋 Welcome: Welcome people at the door, check and sell tickets, and count how many people come in.
- 🧹 Clean: Pack up after the dance: collect the candles and decorations, clear away the water and snacks, empty full bins, close the movable walls, open the curtains, and do the final check.

## 🎟️ Tickets

When you sign up for a task, you get a free ticket for that night. Show it at the door. It is the first button in your sign-up confirmation, and the Me tab lists all your tickets.

## 🎁 Credits

You earn 1 free-entry credit for every 2 tasks you complete. To spend a credit, ask the bot for a free-entry ticket, or use the web page. You then get a ticket for the night you choose. Some nights (sold out, or special events) do not accept credits.

## ⚖️ Tickets and credits

- 🎫 Your working ticket covers all your tasks on a night. It is personal: it can't be given away. Its code stays the same, so a screenshot or a saved QR code keeps working.
- 🎟️ A free-entry ticket can't be cancelled, but you can give it to someone else.
- 💳 A bought ticket never turns into a credit.
- 🔁 Taking a task on a night you already have a ticket for changes nothing about that ticket.
- ➕ Each extra task on the same night earns one more credit. It shows as pending and is added after the event, if you did the task.
- 🔹 Free-entry credits and tickets have no cash value. They can't be refunded, sold or paid out in any form; they can only be used to get a ticket for an eligible event.

## 📈 Standing

Your standing comes from how your past tasks went:

- 🔹 100%: Completed the task
- 🔹 90%: Arrived late, did the task
- 🔹 80%: Found a replacement
- 🔹 40%: Cancelled under 48 h before
- 🔹 0%: No-show

Cancelling 48 hours or more before has no effect. Recent tasks count more than old ones. You get a rating after 3 finished tasks. 60% or higher is good standing. Below 60%, you cannot sign up for new tasks until you talk to an organizer.
